Sukuna V. Geto: Who made the better diss track?

The diss track of the century has started. The historical beef between rappers Suguru Geto and Ryomen Sukuna all started with Geto calling Sukuna "The biggest monkey he has ever encountered." Many had witnessed the beef between the two as Sukuna recently dropped his new track labled as "Weakling", which many figured out the track is a diss track towards Geto.

Geto also recently dropped a track called "Monkey" as a response to Sukuna. Netizens are calling this the biggest beef ever yet. We have made an interview with a few individuals that are willing to share their point of views of who would win this beef.

Interviews

So our first interview is with a Mr. Toge Inumaki who would like to say a few words about this matter. "Salmon Tuna Mayo Bonito Flakes Fish Roe.", says Inumaki. Inumaki hopes Sukuna Would win.

Our Next interview is with a Mr. Satoru Gojo, who is a rapper himself. "Honestly, I think both of them suck I'd Probably could with the beef if I jumped in but I think Geto would win.", says Gojo.
